Who we are

An independent growth partner for Indian exporters

BuyGenix Solutions is an independent Indian B2B export-import platform founded to bridge the gap between Indian manufacturers and exporters and verified international buyers.

We provide verified buyer leads, dedicated relationship management, digital marketing support and full export compliance assistance to help Indian businesses compete on the global stage.

We are not a marketplace or an aggregator. We are a team of export consultants who work directly with each member to understand their business and deliver measurable results.

Every member gets a real, named Relationship Manager who stays with them for the full membership and is available on WhatsApp.

Our values

What guides everything we do

Six principles that shape how we work, serve our members and grow together.

01

Verified quality

Every lead is checked before delivery. We never send unqualified or cold leads; our standard is verified, relevant and ready to buy.

02

Human partnership

Every member has a real, named Relationship Manager, a person rather than a system, dedicated to their success and available on WhatsApp.

03

Global ambition

We want Indian products in every corner of the world, and we keep expanding our buyer network across new countries and categories.

04

Measurable results

We track and report performance honestly. Members see how many leads they received, where from, and what came of them.

05

Trust and privacy

We treat every member's business information as confidential. We never sell data and we operate with full transparency.

06

India first

Built by Indians, for Indian businesses. We understand the challenges exporters face because we are part of the same ecosystem.
