What we handle in Agri Export & Digital Platforms

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

APEDA Registration

APEDA registration is required for exporting scheduled agricultural and processed food products, connecting your business to a wide range of agri-export scheme benefits.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your APEDA registration and identifies which scheduled products it covers for your business.

APEDA registration guide

APEDA Unit Registration

Registering your specific manufacturing or processing unit under your APEDA membership is a distinct step from company-level registration, and it's often required before certain export benefits apply.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your unit-level APEDA registration alongside your main membership.

APEDA Traceability Assistance

Product traceability records required for certain APEDA-regulated export categories are increasingly demanded by international buyers as a quality assurance measure.

BuyGenix Solutions sets up the traceability records your specific product category requires.

HortiNet Assistance

HortiNet is APEDA's traceability system specifically for grapes and select horticultural exports, and registration here is a prerequisite for shipping these products to several key markets.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your HortiNet registration and ongoing traceability filings.

Peanut.Net Assistance

Peanut.Net is the dedicated traceability system for groundnut exports, required by buyers in markets with strict aflatoxin and quality monitoring.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your Peanut.Net registration and compliance filings.

Tracenet Assistance

Tracenet is APEDA's broader agri-export traceability platform, and staying current on its filings is important for maintaining your export eligibility.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your Tracenet registration and keeps your filings current.

Meat.Net-related Assistance

Meat.Net registration and compliance is required for meat product exporters, tied closely to food safety and traceability standards in destination markets.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your Meat.Net registration and associated compliance requirements.

Basmati.Net-related Assistance

Basmati.Net is the traceability system specific to Basmati rice exports, protecting the geographic-indication status that Basmati rice carries internationally.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your Basmati.Net registration and traceability compliance.

RCAC Application Assistance

A Registration-cum-Allocation Certificate is required where specific export quotas apply, and missing this can mean losing access to a quota-restricted market.

BuyGenix Solutions files your RCAC application where your product category requires it.

Agriculture Product Testing Coordination

Coordinating lab testing of agricultural products against buyer or regulatory specifications is often the deciding factor in whether a shipment clears a destination market's inspection.

BuyGenix Solutions coordinates the specific lab testing your agri product needs before export.

Phytosanitary Certificate Assistance

A Phytosanitary Certificate, issued after plant health inspection, is mandatory for most plant-based exports and is frequently checked at the destination port.

BuyGenix Solutions coordinates your Phytosanitary Certificate inspection and issuance in time for your shipment.

Health Certificate Assistance

A Health Certificate confirming your product meets safety standards is often required by destination-country food safety authorities before customs clearance.

BuyGenix Solutions coordinates the issuance of your Health Certificate ahead of your shipment date.

Laboratory Testing Coordination

Accredited lab testing for quality or safety certification is frequently a hard requirement before a product can be exported to a regulated market.

BuyGenix Solutions arranges accredited lab testing matched to your product and destination market's requirements.
