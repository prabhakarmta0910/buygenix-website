Banking & Payment Documentation

Getting paid for an export shipment involves its own documentation trail — separate from customs, but just as easy to fall behind on. Here's what our 10 banking and payment documentation services cover, and how BuyGenix Solutions keeps your export payments on track.

← All Registration Categories
10 Services in This Category

AD Bank Documentation Assistance

Your Authorised Dealer bank requires specific documentation for export transactions, and incomplete paperwork here is a common reason for delayed remittance processing.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares the exact documentation your AD bank needs for smooth export transaction processing.

Export Bank Account Documentation

Opening or maintaining an export-designated bank account requires its own documentation set, distinct from a standard current account.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares the documentation needed to open or maintain your export bank account.

Foreign Remittance Documentation

Supporting documentation for incoming foreign remittances from export sales is often requested by your bank before funds are released to your account.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares documentation supporting your foreign remittances so funds clear without unnecessary bank queries.

FIRC Documentation Assistance

A Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate confirms receipt of export payment and is frequently required to claim various export benefits and schemes.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you obtain your FIRC promptly after each export payment is received.

BRC/e-BRC Follow-up Assistance

A Bank Realisation Certificate is required to claim several export benefits, and following up on its generation is a step that's easy to lose track of amid other priorities.

BuyGenix Solutions follows up on your BRC/e-BRC generation so your scheme claims aren't held up.

Export Payment Reconciliation

Reconciling export invoices against actual payments received catches discrepancies before they compound across multiple shipments.

BuyGenix Solutions reconciles your export invoices against payments received on an ongoing basis.

Shipping Bill vs Bank Realisation Reconciliation

Matching shipping bill values against bank realisation records closes out each export transaction cleanly and is often required for scheme compliance.

BuyGenix Solutions reconciles your shipping bill values against bank realisation records for every transaction.

Export Outstanding Payment Tracking

Tracking pending export payments flags overdue realisations before they become a compliance issue with your bank or a cash flow problem for your business.

BuyGenix Solutions tracks your outstanding export payments and flags overdue realisations early.

Bank Query Documentation

Preparing documentation to respond to your bank's queries on an export transaction keeps your banking relationship smooth and your funds moving.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares clear documentation to respond to any bank query on your export transactions.

Export Proceeds Documentation

Confirming that export proceeds have been properly realised and recorded protects you in the event of a future compliance review.

BuyGenix Solutions compiles documentation confirming your export proceeds are properly realised and recorded.
