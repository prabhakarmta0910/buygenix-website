Basic Business Registrations

Every business in India starts with a foundation of core registrations — choosing the right structure, getting your PAN and TAN, and setting up the certificates you'll need for everything that follows. Here's what each of these 20 registrations actually covers, and how BuyGenix Solutions helps you get each one right.

← All Registration Categories
20 Services in This Category

Proprietorship Registration Assistance

A sole proprietorship is the fastest and simplest way to start a business in India, ideal for solo founders who want to begin trading without heavy compliance. This is one of the most searched terms among first-time entrepreneurs comparing proprietorship vs company registration in India.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you register your proprietorship, get your business PAN, and set up the basic documentation banks and GST authorities expect.

Partnership Firm Registration

A registered partnership firm formalises the profit-sharing, roles, and responsibilities between two or more business partners under the Indian Partnership Act, and having a registered deed strengthens your legal standing in any future dispute. Many small exporters and family businesses in India still operate as registered partnership firms for their simplicity.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts your partnership deed, registers it with the Registrar of Firms, and makes sure the profit-sharing terms are documented clearly from day one.

LLP Registration

An LLP registration in India combines partnership-style flexibility with limited liability protection for its partners, making it a popular structure among consultants, service businesses, and small export firms. LLP vs Private Limited Company is one of the most searched comparisons among new business owners deciding how to register.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your complete LLP registration process — name reservation, LLP agreement drafting, and filing with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

Private Limited Company Registration

Private Limited Company registration is the structure most recognised by banks, investors, and large international buyers, making it the preferred choice for businesses planning to scale or raise funding. This is consistently one of the highest-searched business registration terms in India for founders comparing structures before incorporation.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your entire company incorporation — DIN, DSC, name approval, MOA/AOA drafting, and Certificate of Incorporation filing with the ROC.

One Person Company Registration

A One Person Company (OPC) lets a solo founder enjoy limited liability protection and a corporate structure without needing a co-founder or additional shareholder, a common search for entrepreneurs asking how to register a company alone in India.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you evaluate whether an OPC fits your growth plans and manages the full registration process end-to-end.

Section 8 Company Registration

Section 8 company registration is used for non-profit, charitable, or social-purpose organisations, offering tax exemptions and legal recognition without permitting profit distribution to members — a specific structure many NGOs and foundations search for when formalising their operations.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your Section 8 application, objects clause, and supporting documents required for MCA approval.

Company Incorporation Assistance

Company incorporation in India involves several sequential steps — name approval, digital signature, director identification, and MOA/AOA filing — and getting any one wrong can delay your Certificate of Incorporation by weeks. This is one of the most common pain points founders search for guidance on.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your entire incorporation timeline end-to-end so you're not chasing multiple government portals on your own.

Trade Name Registration

Registering your trade name gives your business a recognised identity to invoice and operate under, distinct from your legal entity name, and is a common early step for businesses building a public-facing brand.

BuyGenix Solutions checks name availability and files your trade name registration so your brand and your legal paperwork stay aligned.

Business Name Search

A thorough business name search checks your proposed name against existing company registrations and trademark records before you commit — skipping this step is one of the most common (and costly) mistakes new founders make.

BuyGenix Solutions runs a complete name-availability check across MCA and trademark databases before you file anything.

PAN Application

A business PAN (Permanent Account Number) is the foundation for almost every other registration — GST, bank accounts, and tax filings all require it — making PAN application one of the very first steps in setting up any Indian business.

BuyGenix Solutions files your business PAN application and ensures your supporting documents match what your other registrations will need later.

TAN Application

A TAN (Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number) is mandatory once your business starts deducting TDS on payments like salaries, rent, or professional fees — a requirement many new employers overlook until their first payroll cycle.

BuyGenix Solutions files your TAN application and helps you understand exactly when your business becomes liable to deduct TDS.

MSME/Udyam Registration

Udyam registration (formerly MSME registration) unlocks collateral-free loans, lower interest rates, protection against delayed payments, and subsidies on trademark and patent filings — benefits that make MSME registration India one of the most valuable, and most searched, steps for small business owners.

BuyGenix Solutions completes your Udyam registration for free in a single sitting and explains exactly which MSME benefits your business qualifies for.

Shop & Establishment Registration

Shop & Establishment registration is a state-level requirement for most business premises in India, and it's frequently needed before you can open a current bank account or legally hire your first employee.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your Shop & Establishment registration according to your specific state's rules and timelines.

Professional Tax Registration

Professional tax is a state-level levy on businesses and salaried employees in states where it applies, and missing this registration is a common compliance gap for businesses expanding into a new state.

BuyGenix Solutions checks whether professional tax applies in your state and completes the registration correctly.

Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)

A Digital Signature Certificate is what makes your GST returns, company filings, and DGFT applications legally valid online — without a valid DSC, most government portals simply won't accept your submission.

BuyGenix Solutions arranges your DSC issuance and helps you install it correctly on the device you'll actually use for filing.

Class 3 DSC

A Class 3 DSC is the highest-assurance digital signature certificate, required specifically for e-tendering, e-bidding, and select high-value government filings where a lower-assurance signature isn't accepted.

BuyGenix Solutions arranges your Class 3 DSC when your tender or filing requirement specifically calls for it.

DSC Renewal

A Digital Signature Certificate typically expires after one or two years, and an expired DSC will silently block your GST, MCA, or DGFT filings until it's renewed — a deadline many businesses lose track of.

BuyGenix Solutions tracks your DSC expiry date and handles the renewal before it lapses.

DSC Token Assistance

Your Digital Signature Certificate is issued on a physical USB token, and driver or configuration issues with this token are one of the most common reasons filings fail at the last minute.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you set up your DSC token correctly and troubleshoots it if your filing software isn't recognising it.

Business Address Documentation

Valid business address proof — a rent agreement, utility bill, or ownership document — is required across nearly every registration, from GST to company incorporation, and inconsistent address documents are a frequent cause of application rejection.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares consistent, compliant address documentation that holds up across all your different registration filings.

NOC/Consent Letter Preparation

A No Objection Certificate from your property owner is required whenever you register a business at a rented or shared address, and a poorly drafted NOC is a common reason GST and company registration applications get stuck.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts a properly worded NOC that satisfies GST and MCA documentation requirements the first time.
