What we build

Modules for your ERP

Pick the modules your business needs. Every ERP is built to your process, so you only pay for what you will use.

Requirement study and process mapping

We study how orders, stock, production and accounts move through your business today, and write down exactly what the ERP must do before any build starts.

Sales, quotations and orders

Customer records, quotations, proforma invoices and sales orders in one place, with the status of every order visible to your team.

Export documents from order data

Commercial invoice and packing list generated from the order, with HS codes, Incoterm, ports, weights and buyer details, so the same data is not typed twice.

GST invoicing and accounts

GST invoices with HSN codes and tax summaries that support your return filing, plus ledgers for customers, suppliers and expenses.

Inventory and warehouse

Stock by item and location, batch or lot tracking where you need it, and alerts when stock runs low.

Purchase and supplier management

Purchase orders, supplier records and goods received, linked to stock and to the orders they serve.

Production and costing

Bills of materials, work orders and product costing for manufacturers, so you know the real cost of each order.

Payments and receivables

Track what each buyer has paid and what is outstanding, including export payments against shipments.

Reports and dashboards

Sales, margin, stock and export reports for owners and managers, built around the numbers you actually check.

User roles and access

Separate logins for your staff, with each person seeing only the parts of the system their role needs.

Browser and mobile access

Runs in a web browser, so your team can use it from the office, the warehouse or a phone.

Data migration

Your existing customers, items and opening balances moved in from spreadsheets or from your current software, where it allows export.

FAQ

Custom ERP: common questions

What is a custom ERP?

ERP (enterprise resource planning) software brings sales, purchase, stock, production, accounts and reports into one system. A custom ERP is built around how your business works, instead of changing your process to fit a ready-made product.

Should I choose custom ERP or ready-made accounting software?

Ready-made software suits businesses with standard needs. A custom ERP makes sense when your process is specific, for example export orders with their own documents, production steps, or reports that ready-made tools do not give you. We will tell you honestly in the consultation which fits your business better.

How much does a custom ERP cost?

The price depends on the modules, the number of users and how much data needs to be moved. After the requirement study you get a written scope and price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. The timeline is part of the written quote, and we build in stages so you can start using the first modules early.

Who owns the software and the data?

Ownership of the data, hosting and access are agreed in writing before the project starts, so there are no surprises later.

Can it work with my current accounting software?

During the requirement study we check what your current software can import and export, and plan the ERP around it.

Is ERP included in a BuyGenix membership?

No. Custom ERP projects are quoted separately from membership plans.
