Overview

What is custom ERP software?

ERP (enterprise resource planning) software connects the parts of a business that usually live in separate spreadsheets: enquiries and quotations, sales orders, purchase, inventory, production, invoicing, payments and reports. Every department works from the same data, so a sales order updates stock, the invoice uses the same customer and item details, and the owner sees the result in one dashboard.

A custom ERP is built for your business instead of being bought off the shelf. You decide the modules, the screens, the approval steps and the reports. For exporters this means export documents generated straight from the order; for manufacturers it means production and costing that follow the way your factory actually runs.

BuyGenix builds custom ERP software for exporters, manufacturers, traders and MSMEs in India. Because we already help exporters with registrations, documentation and buyers, we understand the paperwork and the questions an export business deals with every day.

Is it time?

Signs your business needs an ERP

If several of these sound familiar, your business has likely outgrown spreadsheets and basic billing software.

  • The same customer or order is typed into several spreadsheets
  • Stock in the books does not match stock in the godown
  • Export documents take hours to prepare for every shipment
  • You cannot see which buyers owe money and for how long
  • Costing is a guess, so you are not sure which orders make a profit
  • Only one person knows how the current system works
  • Discount and purchase approvals happen on calls or chat, with no record
  • Ready-made software forces you to change how your business runs

FAQ

Custom ERP software: common questions

What is ERP software?

ERP stands for enterprise resource planning. ERP software brings sales, purchase, inventory, production, accounts and reports into one system, so every department works from the same data.

What is a custom ERP?

A custom ERP is built around how your business works, instead of changing your process to fit a ready-made product. You choose the modules, screens and reports your team needs.

Is ERP useful for small businesses and MSMEs?

Yes. An ERP does not have to be large or expensive. Many MSMEs start with two or three modules, such as orders, stock and invoicing, and add more as they grow.

Should I choose custom ERP or ready-made accounting software?

Ready-made software suits businesses with standard needs. A custom ERP makes sense when your process is specific, for example export orders with their own documents, production steps, or reports that ready-made tools do not give you. We will tell you honestly in the consultation which fits your business better.

Can the ERP create export documents?

Yes. Export documentation can be one of the modules: commercial invoice, packing list and proforma invoice generated from the order, with HS codes, Incoterm, ports and buyer details.

Does it handle GST?

GST invoicing with HSN codes and tax summaries can be built in, so your records support return filing. Your accountant or GST professional still files the returns.

Can it work in more than one currency?

Yes. Multi-currency quotes and invoices can be included, with the rupee value recorded for your accounts.

Is it cloud-based?

The ERP runs in a web browser, so your team can use it from any location. Hosting is agreed in writing before the project starts.

How much does a custom ERP cost?

The price depends on the modules, the number of users and how much data needs to be moved. After the requirement study you get a written scope and price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. The timeline is part of the written quote, and we build in stages so you can start using the first modules early.

Who owns the software and the data?

Ownership of the data, hosting and access are agreed in writing before the project starts, so there are no surprises later.

Can it work with my current accounting software?

During the requirement study we check what your current software can import and export, and plan the ERP around it.

No. Custom ERP projects are quoted separately from membership plans.
