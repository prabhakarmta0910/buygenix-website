What we handle in DGFT & IEC Services

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

IEC Registration

An IEC (Import Export Code) is mandatory before any import or export shipment can legally clear customs — IEC registration India is one of the very first steps for any new exporter.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your complete IEC registration on the DGFT portal so your first shipment isn't held up waiting on paperwork.

IEC code registration guide

IEC Modification

Modifying your IEC — for address, business name, or ownership changes — keeps your registration consistent with your other filings, and mismatches here can cause customs and banking issues later.

BuyGenix Solutions files your IEC modification accurately, keeping every linked registration in sync.

IEC Update/Annual Confirmation

DGFT requires every IEC holder to confirm and update their details annually between April and June, even with no changes — missing this window can get your IEC deactivated without warning.

BuyGenix Solutions tracks your IEC annual confirmation deadline so your export operations are never interrupted.

IEC Reactivation Assistance

A deactivated IEC blocks every future shipment until it's reactivated, and reactivation often takes longer than the original registration did.

BuyGenix Solutions manages the IEC reactivation process quickly to minimise disruption to your export schedule.

IEC Surrender

Formally surrendering an IEC that's no longer needed closes it out cleanly with DGFT, avoiding confusion in future compliance checks.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your IEC surrender with the correct DGFT paperwork.

IEC Profile Correction

Errors in your IEC profile — a wrong PAN link, incorrect address, or outdated bank detail — can silently block shipments or banking transactions until corrected.

BuyGenix Solutions reviews and corrects your IEC profile data before it causes a shipment delay.

IEC Bank Account Update

Updating the bank account linked to your IEC is required whenever you change your export banking relationship, and skipping it can disrupt your incentive claims and remittance processing.

BuyGenix Solutions updates your IEC bank details promptly whenever your banking arrangement changes.

IEC Address Update

Keeping your IEC address current with your actual business premises avoids mismatches that customs and banks may flag during document checks.

BuyGenix Solutions updates your IEC address in line with your other registration filings.

IEC Branch Addition

Adding a branch location to your IEC is necessary as your export operations expand to new premises or warehouses.

BuyGenix Solutions files your branch addition correctly so all your locations are properly reflected on your IEC.

IEC Certificate Download

Your official IEC certificate is often requested by banks and buyers as proof of your export registration, and having it readily available speeds up onboarding with new partners.

BuyGenix Solutions retrieves and provides your IEC certificate whenever your bank or buyer needs it.

DGFT Login/Profile Assistance

DGFT portal access issues are a common frustration for exporters trying to file authorisations or check policy — profile setup problems can delay time-sensitive applications.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you set up and troubleshoot your DGFT portal access so filings aren't delayed by login issues.

DGFT DSC Mapping

Mapping your Digital Signature Certificate to your DGFT profile is required before you can file any authenticated application, and a mismatched DSC is a common technical blocker.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your DGFT DSC mapping correctly so your applications go through without technical rejection.

Digital Signature Certificate guide

DGFT Application Filing

General DGFT applications cover a range of authorisations and confirmations, each with its own document requirements — getting the format wrong is a frequent cause of processing delays.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares and files your DGFT applications with the correct format and supporting documents.

DGFT Query/Deficiency Reply

A deficiency memo from DGFT needs a precise, complete response within the given timeline, or your pending application risks rejection.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts a clear, complete reply to your DGFT deficiency memo to keep your application moving.

DGFT Authorisation Applications

Authorisations like Advance Authorisation and EPCG are filed through DGFT and carry export obligation conditions that need to be understood before you apply.

BuyGenix Solutions files your DGFT authorisation applications and explains the obligations that come attached to them.

DGFT Export Policy Checking

Export policy for a product can change through a DGFT notification with little advance notice, so checking current policy status before committing to a shipment protects you from a blocked consignment.

BuyGenix Solutions checks your product's current export policy status against the latest DGFT notifications before you ship.

ITC-HS Code Identification

Correct ITC-HS code identification determines your product's tariff treatment, export policy status, and eligibility for incentive schemes — misclassification is one of the costliest, most common exporter mistakes.

BuyGenix Solutions identifies the correct ITC-HS code for your product so your classification holds up under customs and DGFT scrutiny.

Product Export Policy Check

Confirming whether your specific product is freely exportable, restricted, or prohibited avoids the costly surprise of a shipment being held at the port.

BuyGenix Solutions verifies your product's export policy status before you commit to a shipment date.

Restricted Item Export Assistance

Exporting a restricted-category item requires additional licensing beyond a standard IEC, and the application process varies significantly by product.

BuyGenix Solutions guides you through the specific licensing process required for your restricted-category product.

Export License Application Assistance

Products requiring specific DGFT export licensing need a carefully prepared application, since incomplete filings are routinely sent back for clarification.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares and files your export licence application with the complete documentation DGFT expects.
