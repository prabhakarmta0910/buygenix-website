What you get

A buyer lead is a real purchase requirement, not a directory listing

Directories give you a list of companies to cold-call. A buyer lead tells you who wants to buy, what, how much and where it has to ship, so your first message can be a quotation.

Matched to your products

Leads are tagged by category and product, so you search only what you make or trade: spices, rice, textiles, engineering goods, handicrafts, furniture and more.

Limited sharing

A lead is shared with at most 5 sellers. The buyer gets a few serious quotes instead of dozens, and you are not one name in a crowd.

Direct contact

Once unlocked you get the buyer's name, mobile number and email and talk to them yourself. No middleman, no commission on your deal.

Global and domestic buyers

Requirements come from importers in the UAE, UK, USA, Canada, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Oman and other markets, plus bulk buyers across India.

Saved in your portal

Every lead you unlock stays in your client portal with its contact details, so your team can follow up and track each enquiry.

Relationship Manager

Your RM sets up your business listing, shares matched leads on WhatsApp and email, and helps with export registrations when you need them.

Tips to convert a buyer lead

  1. Reply the same day. Buyers usually decide among the first few quotes.
  2. Quote the exact product, grade and quantity asked for, with Incoterm (FOB or CIF) and validity.
  3. Share registrations that matter to the buyer: IEC, RCMC, FSSAI, APEDA or Spices Board where relevant.
  4. Offer samples and a short product catalogue with photos.
  5. Agree safe payment terms such as an advance or a letter of credit.

Questions about buyer leads

What are export buyer leads?

An export buyer lead is a live purchase requirement from an importer or bulk buyer: the product, quantity and destination, plus the buyer's contact details. Suppliers use it to send a quotation directly to someone who is already looking to buy.

How does BuyGenix verify buyer leads?

Our team checks each requirement before it is listed: that the product and quantity are clear, the buyer can be reached, and the location is real. Leads that cannot be confirmed are not shared.

Can I see buyer leads before paying?

Yes. The product, quantity and buyer location of every listed requirement are free to browse on the Buyers and Buy Lead Search pages. Only the buyer's name, mobile number and email need a membership.

How many suppliers receive the same lead?

Each lead is shared with at most 5 sellers, so the buyer receives a few serious quotes and you are not competing with a crowd.

Do I need a membership to contact buyers?

Yes. Buyer contact details unlock only with lead credits, and lead credits come with a BuyGenix membership plan. Free accounts can browse requirements but cannot unlock contacts.

How many leads come with each plan?

Starter includes 5 lead credits a year, Growth 10, Professional 15 and Enterprise 30. One credit unlocks one buyer's full contact. Every plan also includes a dedicated Relationship Manager.

Which countries do the buyers come from?

Requirements come from importers in the UAE, United Kingdom, USA, Canada, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Oman and other markets, as well as bulk buyers across India.

Is BuyGenix a buyer generation service or a B2B directory?

BuyGenix is a buyer generation service. Instead of a list of companies to cold-call, you get specific purchase requirements and contact the buyer directly, with no commission on your deal.
