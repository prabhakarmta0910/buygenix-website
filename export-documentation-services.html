Export Documentation

A single missing or mismatched document can hold up a shipment or delay payment on a Letter of Credit. Here's what our 20 export documentation services cover, from the first proforma invoice to final clearance, and how BuyGenix Solutions keeps every document consistent.

Proforma Invoice Preparation

A proforma invoice confirms order terms with your buyer ahead of the actual shipment, and getting the details right avoids disputes once the real invoice is raised.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts your proforma invoice to match exactly what you and your buyer have agreed.

Commercial Invoice Preparation

The commercial invoice is required for both customs clearance and buyer payment, making accuracy here critical to avoiding delays on both fronts.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your commercial invoice to satisfy both customs and your buyer's payment requirements.

Packing List Preparation

A packing list that precisely matches your shipment's actual contents and packaging is essential — mismatches here are a common reason for customs queries.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares a precise packing list that matches your actual shipment contents.

Purchase Order Drafting

BuyGenix Solutions drafts purchase order documentation reflecting your agreed transaction terms.

Sales Contract Drafting

BuyGenix Solutions drafts sales contracts that protect your interests on price, delivery, and payment terms.

Export Agreement Drafting

Letter of Credit Documentation

BuyGenix Solutions reviews your export documents against your LC terms line by line to avoid discrepancy-related payment delays.

Bill of Lading Documentation Assistance

BuyGenix Solutions prepares supporting documentation that matches your Bill of Lading accurately for ocean freight.

Airway Bill Documentation Assistance

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your air shipment documentation to match your Airway Bill precisely.

Certificate of Origin Assistance

A Certificate of Origin confirms where your goods were manufactured, and it's often required by customs authorities or trade agreement terms in the destination country.

BuyGenix Solutions processes your Certificate of Origin application accurately and on time.

Non-Preferential Certificate of Origin

A standard, non-preferential Certificate of Origin confirms origin without claiming any trade agreement tariff benefit, and is required by many destination countries regardless of trade deals.

BuyGenix Solutions issues your non-preferential Certificate of Origin for shipments that don't involve a trade agreement claim.

Preferential Certificate of Origin

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your preferential Certificate of Origin correctly so your buyer can actually claim the tariff benefit.

BuyGenix Solutions files your e-CoO application accurately through the electronic system.

BuyGenix Solutions coordinates your inspection and prepares the resulting certificate to satisfy buyer or LC terms.
