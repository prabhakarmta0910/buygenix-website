Which Export Documentation services does BuyGenix handle?

We handle 20 services in this category, including Proforma Invoice Preparation, Commercial Invoice Preparation, Packing List Preparation, Purchase Order Drafting, Sales Contract Drafting. Our team prepares the documents, files the application and follows up until it is approved.

How does the process work?

It takes five steps: a free consultation, a document checklist for your case, filing by our team, follow-up on any department queries, and delivery of the certificate or number.

What documents do I need?

Each service has its own checklist. Most start with the PAN card of the owner or business, proof of business address, a bank statement or cancelled cheque, the Aadhaar card of the authorised signatory and a passport-size photograph. Companies and firms also need an authorisation letter and a Digital Signature Certificate. We share the exact list once we know your business.
