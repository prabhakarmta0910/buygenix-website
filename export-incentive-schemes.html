Export Incentive Schemes

Export incentive schemes can meaningfully improve your margins — but only if you claim the ones you're actually eligible for and track the obligations that come with them. Here's what our 12 export incentive scheme services cover.

← All Registration Categories
12 Services in This Category

RoDTEP Claim Assistance

Files RoDTEP claims to recover embedded taxes and duties on your exported products.

Duty Drawback Documentation

Prepares documentation to claim Duty Drawback on customs duty paid on imported inputs used in exports.

Advance Authorisation Assistance

Files Advance Authorisation applications for duty-free import of inputs used in export production.

EPCG Application Assistance

Files EPCG applications for concessional-duty import of capital goods tied to an export obligation.

Export Obligation Tracking

Tracks your export obligation timelines under schemes like EPCG and Advance Authorisation.

DGFT Licence Amendment

Files amendments to an existing DGFT licence or authorisation.

Licence Closure Assistance

Manages the closure process once your export obligation under a scheme licence is fulfilled.

Redemption Application Assistance

Files redemption applications confirming fulfilment of scheme obligations to DGFT.

EODC Assistance

Obtains the Export Obligation Discharge Certificate confirming your scheme obligations are met.

Export Incentive Reconciliation

Reconciles claimed incentives across schemes against actual shipments and realisations.

Scheme Eligibility Check

Checks which export incentive schemes your business and product actually qualify for.

Export Promotion Scheme Documentation

Prepares the documentation set required to apply for a specific export promotion scheme.
