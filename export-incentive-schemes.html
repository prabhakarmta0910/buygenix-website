Export Incentive Schemes

Export incentive schemes can meaningfully improve your margins — but only if you claim the ones you're actually eligible for and track the obligations that come with them. Here's what our 12 export incentive scheme services cover, and how BuyGenix Solutions makes sure nothing goes unclaimed.

What we handle in Export Incentive Schemes

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

RoDTEP Claim Assistance

RoDTEP recovers embedded taxes and duties on exported products, and claim rates vary by HS code — getting your classification right directly affects how much you recover.

BuyGenix Solutions files your RoDTEP claims accurately, matched to the correct rate for your product's HS code.

RoDTEP updates 2026

Duty Drawback Documentation

Duty Drawback recovers customs duty paid on imported inputs used in your export production, and is especially valuable for exporters relying on imported raw materials.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your Duty Drawback documentation to maximise your legitimate claim.

Duty Drawback guide

Advance Authorisation Assistance

Advance Authorisation allows duty-free import of inputs used in export production, but comes with an export obligation that needs to be tracked carefully.

BuyGenix Solutions files your Advance Authorisation application and helps you track the attached export obligation.

Advance Authorisation guide

EPCG Application Assistance

EPCG allows concessional-duty import of capital goods tied to an export obligation, making it worth evaluating before any major equipment investment.

BuyGenix Solutions files your EPCG application and helps you plan around the resulting export obligation.

EPCG scheme guide

Export Obligation Tracking

Tracking your export obligation timelines under schemes like EPCG and Advance Authorisation is essential — missing a deadline can trigger penalties on the duty you saved.

BuyGenix Solutions tracks your scheme export obligations so deadlines never catch you by surprise.

DGFT Licence Amendment

Amending an existing DGFT licence or authorisation is sometimes necessary as your business or shipment plans change after the original filing.

BuyGenix Solutions files DGFT licence amendments accurately when your circumstances change.

Licence Closure Assistance

Closing out a scheme licence once your export obligation is fulfilled is a formal process that needs proper documentation to avoid future compliance flags.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your licence closure process once your obligations are met.

Redemption Application Assistance

A redemption application formally confirms to DGFT that your scheme obligations have been met, closing the loop on your authorisation.

BuyGenix Solutions files your redemption application with the documentation DGFT requires.

EODC Assistance

An Export Obligation Discharge Certificate is the formal confirmation that your scheme obligations are complete, and obtaining it protects you from future compliance queries.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you obtain your EODC once your export obligations are fulfilled.

Export Incentive Reconciliation

Reconciling claimed incentives across schemes against actual shipments and realisations catches any gaps before they become compliance issues.

BuyGenix Solutions reconciles your export incentive claims against your actual shipment records.

Scheme Eligibility Check

Checking which export incentive schemes your business and product actually qualify for prevents both missed opportunities and invalid claims.

BuyGenix Solutions checks your eligibility across every relevant export incentive scheme so nothing goes unclaimed.

Export Promotion Scheme Documentation

The documentation set required to apply for a specific export promotion scheme varies by scheme, and incomplete filings are a common cause of rejected applications.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares the exact documentation your chosen export scheme requires.
