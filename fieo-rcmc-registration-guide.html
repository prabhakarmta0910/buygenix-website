FIEO RCMC Registration for Exporters: Complete Guide

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 7 min read
Last updated 6 October 2026
Quick answer

An RCMC (Registration-cum-Membership Certificate) is proof that an exporter is registered with an export promotion council. FIEO, the Federation of Indian Export Organisations, issues RCMCs to multi-product and merchant exporters and to exporters whose products are not covered by a product-specific council. You need a valid RCMC to claim many Foreign Trade Policy benefits, and it is applied for online through the DGFT portal.

What is an RCMC?

Under the Foreign Trade Policy, exporters who want to claim benefits such as Advance Authorisation or EPCG must be registered with the relevant export promotion council. The council issues an RCMC, which records your membership, products and exporter category.

When should you register with FIEO?

  • You export many unrelated products, as a merchant or trading exporter
  • Your product is not covered by a product-specific export promotion council
  • You want FIEO's export services, trade fairs, buyer-seller meets and market information
If a product-specific council covers your main product, for example EEPC India for engineering goods or AEPC for apparel, register there instead or as well.

Documents you need

  • Importer Exporter Code (IEC)
  • PAN and GST registration
  • Proof of business constitution and address
  • Bank certificate or cancelled cheque
  • Details of products exported or planned, and export turnover if any
  • Digital signature of the authorised signatory

How to apply

  1. Log in to the DGFT portalUse the account linked to your IEC.
  2. Choose the RCMC serviceSelect FIEO as the export promotion council.
  3. Fill the applicationEnter products, exporter category and business details, and upload documents.
  4. Pay the membership feeThe fee depends on your category and is shown during the application.
  5. Get the RCMCAfter FIEO approves the application, the RCMC is issued electronically.

Frequently asked questions

Is an RCMC mandatory for exporting?

Not for every shipment, but you need a valid RCMC to claim many Foreign Trade Policy benefits, such as Advance Authorisation and EPCG.

Who should take RCMC from FIEO?

Multi-product and merchant exporters, and exporters whose products are not covered by a product-specific export promotion council.

How long is an RCMC valid?

Generally five years. Renew it before expiry to keep claiming benefits.

Where do I apply for an RCMC?

Online through the DGFT portal, choosing the relevant export promotion council.
