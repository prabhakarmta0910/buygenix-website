How to Find Verified Suppliers in India: A Canada Buyer's Sourcing Guide

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read

Searching "supplier India" from Canada returns thousands of listings — and a genuinely useful fraction of them. For Canada buyers new to sourcing from India, the real challenge isn't finding suppliers, it's finding ones worth actually doing business with.

Why a Long Contact List Isn't the Same as a Good Sourcing Strategy

Many Canada buyers start by messaging dozens of listings on open directories, only to find that a large share never respond, quote inconsistent prices, or turn out to be trading intermediaries rather than actual manufacturers. A shorter list of verified, genuinely capable suppliers consistently outperforms a long list of unverified contacts — every conversation is with someone who can actually fulfil the order.

What to Check Before You Commit to an Indian Supplier

  • Valid IEC and GST registration — ask for these directly; a legitimate exporter will share them without hesitation
  • Manufacturing capability versus trading — confirm whether you're dealing with the actual manufacturer or a reseller, since pricing and lead times differ significantly
  • Export track record — ask for references from existing international buyers, ideally in a market comparable to Canada
  • Sample consistency — a sample that looks great means little if the supplier can't replicate that quality at production volume

The Canada-Specific Sourcing Challenge

For Canada buyers specifically, one of the most common friction points is confirming an Indian supplier's documentation meets both CBSA and Canadian product-safety requirements. This is exactly the kind of verification work that's difficult to do remotely without someone on the ground in India who can check documentation and manufacturing capability directly. Shipments into Canada are cleared through Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA), so your Indian supplier's paperwork needs to hold up to that scrutiny from the first shipment.
