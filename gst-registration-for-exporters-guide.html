GST Registration for Exporters in India: A Complete Step-by-Step Guide

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 7 min read

If you're planning to sell your products to international buyers, GST registration for exporters in India is one of the first legal steps you cannot skip. Whether you're a manufacturer, a trading company, or a small business owner just starting out in export, understanding the GST registration process for export business is essential before you raise your first international invoice.

Who Needs GST Registration for Export Business in India

Unlike regular domestic businesses that have a turnover threshold before GST becomes mandatory, any business engaged in export of goods or services from India is required to register under GST, regardless of turnover. This is because exports are treated as inter-state supply under Indian tax law, and inter-state suppliers must register for GST from day one.

Documents Required for GST Registration for Exporters

Before starting the GST registration process for your export business, keep the following documents ready:

  • PAN card of the business or proprietor
  • Aadhaar card of the authorized signatory
  • Proof of business registration (Partnership Deed, Certificate of Incorporation, or Udyam/MSME registration)
  • Address proof of the principal place of business (electricity bill, rent agreement, or NOC from owner)
  • Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
  • Digital signature (DSC) for companies and LLPs
  • Passport-size photograph of the applicant

Step-by-Step GST Registration Process for Export Business

The GST registration process for exporters is completed entirely online through the GST portal:

  • Step 1: Visit the GST portal and generate a Temporary Reference Number (TRN) using your PAN, mobile number, and email.
  • Step 2: Log in with the TRN and complete Part B of the application with business details, promoter details, and principal place of business.
  • Step 3: Upload all required documents in the prescribed format.
  • Step 4: Verify the application using DSC, e-Sign, or EVC (OTP-based verification).
  • Step 5: Once verified, you'll receive an Application Reference Number (ARN) to track your application status.
  • Step 6: On approval, your GSTIN (GST Identification Number) is issued, usually within 3–7 working days.

What is LUT (Letter of Undertaking) and Why Exporters Need It

Once registered, most exporters also need to file a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) under GST. Filing an LUT allows you to export goods or services without paying IGST upfront — this is what makes exports "zero-rated" under GST. Without an LUT, you would need to pay IGST on every export shipment and then separately claim a refund, which ties up working capital unnecessarily. The LUT is valid for one financial year and must be renewed annually.

How GST Refunds Work for Zero-Rated Exports

Even with an LUT in place, exporters often accumulate Input Tax Credit (ITC) on raw materials, packaging, and services used in producing export goods. This accumulated ITC can be claimed as a GST refund for exporters through the GST portal. Timely and accurate GST refund filing is critical for maintaining healthy cash flow, especially for small and mid-sized export businesses where working capital is often tight.

Getting GST registration, LUT filing, and refund claims right the first time saves significant time and avoids compliance notices down the line. This is exactly where working with an experienced registration partner makes a real difference.
