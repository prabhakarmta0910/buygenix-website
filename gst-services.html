GST Services

GST touches almost every part of running a business in India — from initial registration through monthly filings, refunds, and the occasional department notice. Here's what each of our 20 GST services actually involves.

← All Registration Categories
20 Services in This Category

GST Registration

Registers your business under GST so you can legally invoice, collect tax, and claim input tax credit.

GST Amendment

Updates your GST registration details — address, business name, or authorised signatory — through the official amendment process.

GST Cancellation

Formally closes a GST registration that's no longer needed, avoiding penalties for non-filing on a dormant registration.

GST Revocation

Restores a GST registration that was cancelled by the department, typically after resolving the underlying compliance issue.

GST Registration Restoration Assistance

Helps reinstate a lapsed or suspended GST registration so your business can resume normal invoicing.

GST Return Filing – GSTR-1

Files your monthly or quarterly outward supply return, reporting sales invoices to the GST portal.

GST Return Filing – GSTR-3B

Files your summary GST return declaring tax liability and input tax credit for the period.

Nil GST Return Filing

Files a GST return for a period with no business activity, keeping your registration compliant even when there's nothing to report.

GSTR-9 Annual Return

Files your consolidated annual GST return summarising the full financial year's transactions.

GSTR-9C Assistance

Prepares the reconciliation statement required alongside GSTR-9 for businesses above the applicable turnover threshold.

LUT Filing

Files a Letter of Undertaking so you can export goods or services without paying IGST upfront.

LUT Renewal

Renews your LUT for the new financial year, since it's valid for one year at a time.

GST Refund Application

Files a refund claim for accumulated input tax credit or excess GST paid.

Export GST Refund Assistance

Prepares and files GST refund claims specific to zero-rated export supplies.

RFD-01 Filing

Files Form RFD-01, the standard application form used for most categories of GST refund claims.

GST Notice Reply Drafting

Drafts a formal, documented reply to a GST department notice or query.

GST Registration Clarification Reply

Responds to a clarification query raised by the department during GST registration processing.

GST Practitioner/Consultancy Assistance

Provides ongoing GST advisory support for return filing, classification, and compliance questions.

GST Reconciliation

Matches your GST returns against your books and GSTR-2B/2A to catch mismatches before they trigger notices.

Input Tax Credit Reconciliation

Reconciles the input tax credit claimed in your returns against what your suppliers have actually reported.
