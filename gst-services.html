GST Services

What we handle in GST Services

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

GST Registration

GST registration is mandatory for any business crossing the turnover threshold, and mandatory from day one for exporters — GST registration process India is one of the most frequently searched compliance topics for new business owners.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your complete GST registration, from document preparation to ARN tracking until your GSTIN is issued.

GST registration for exporters guide

GST Amendment

A GST amendment updates your registration details — address, business name, or authorised signatory — and filing it incorrectly can create mismatches that trigger department notices later.

BuyGenix Solutions files your GST amendment accurately so your registration details stay consistent across every filing.

GST Cancellation

Formally cancelling an unused GST registration prevents accumulating late fees and notices on a dormant number — a step many closed or paused businesses forget to complete.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your GST cancellation cleanly, including any final return filings the department requires.

GST Revocation

GST revocation restores a registration that was cancelled by the department, typically due to non-filing, and must be requested within a strict window after cancellation.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you file for revocation quickly and resolve the underlying compliance gap that caused the cancellation.

GST Registration Restoration Assistance

Restoring a suspended or lapsed GST registration involves clearing pending returns and dues first — a process that can stall if the paperwork isn't complete.

BuyGenix Solutions manages the full restoration process so your business can resume normal invoicing without unnecessary delay.

GST Return Filing – GSTR-1

GSTR-1 is your monthly or quarterly outward supply return, reporting every sales invoice to the GST portal — accuracy here directly affects your buyers' input tax credit.

BuyGenix Solutions files your GSTR-1 on time, every period, with your invoice data properly reconciled beforehand.

GST Return Filing – GSTR-3B

GSTR-3B is the summary return declaring your tax liability and input tax credit for the period, and it's one of the most time-sensitive GST filings on the calendar.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares and files your GSTR-3B accurately and on schedule, every single period.

Nil GST Return Filing

Even with zero business activity in a period, a Nil GST return still needs to be filed to keep your registration compliant — skipping it is a surprisingly common cause of late fees.

BuyGenix Solutions makes sure your Nil returns are filed on time so a quiet month never turns into a compliance problem.

GSTR-9 Annual Return

GSTR-9 consolidates your full financial year's GST transactions into a single annual return, and reconciling a year's worth of data is where most errors surface.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your GSTR-9 with careful reconciliation against your monthly filings before submission.

GSTR-9 annual return guide

GSTR-9C Assistance

GSTR-9C is the reconciliation statement required alongside GSTR-9 for businesses above the applicable turnover threshold, and it demands close matching between your books and your GST returns.

BuyGenix Solutions supports your GSTR-9C preparation with a thorough books-to-returns reconciliation.

LUT Filing

Filing an LUT (Letter of Undertaking) lets you export without paying IGST upfront, which is what makes exports genuinely zero-rated under GST — LUT filing for exporters is a frequently searched compliance step.

BuyGenix Solutions files your LUT correctly and on time so your exports stay duty-free from day one of the financial year.

GST LUT filing guide

LUT Renewal

Your LUT is valid for only one financial year, so it must be renewed annually — missing this renewal means paying IGST upfront on every export until it's refiled.

BuyGenix Solutions tracks your LUT's annual renewal deadline so your export invoicing is never interrupted.

GST Refund Application

A GST refund application recovers accumulated input tax credit or excess tax paid, and incomplete documentation is the single biggest reason refund claims get delayed.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares a complete, well-documented refund application to minimise back-and-forth with the department.

Export GST Refund Assistance

GST refund for exporters specifically targets the input tax credit accumulated on zero-rated export supplies, and getting this refund processed quickly matters directly for your working capital.

BuyGenix Solutions specialises in export-specific GST refund filing, keeping your claims accurate and your cash flow moving.

RFD-01 Filing

Form RFD-01 is the standard application used for most categories of GST refund claims, and its accompanying statements need to match your return data precisely.

BuyGenix Solutions files your RFD-01 with all supporting statements correctly reconciled against your GST returns.

GST Notice Reply Drafting

A well-drafted, evidence-backed reply to a GST department notice can be the difference between a closed matter and an escalated dispute — GST notice reply drafting is a service many businesses seek out under time pressure.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts a clear, documented reply to your GST notice, addressing the department's specific query point by point.

GST Registration Clarification Reply

When the department raises a clarification query during GST registration processing, a prompt and complete reply is essential to avoid your application being rejected outright.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts your clarification reply with the exact supporting documents the query is asking for.

GST Practitioner/Consultancy Assistance

Ongoing GST advisory support helps you stay ahead of classification changes, filing deadlines, and rate updates — especially valuable given how often GST rules have shifted in recent years.

BuyGenix Solutions provides continuous GST consultancy so you're never caught off guard by a rule change.

GST Reconciliation

GST reconciliation matches your filed returns against your own books and your suppliers' GSTR-2B/2A data, catching mismatches before they turn into a department notice.

BuyGenix Solutions runs regular GST reconciliation so discrepancies are caught and fixed long before they become a compliance issue.

Input Tax Credit Reconciliation

Input tax credit reconciliation checks the ITC you've claimed against what your suppliers have actually reported to the department — a growing area of GST scrutiny in recent years.

BuyGenix Solutions reconciles your ITC claims against supplier filings to protect you from credit reversal notices.

GST Services: common questions

Which GST services does BuyGenix handle?

We handle 20 services in this category, including GST Registration, GST Amendment, GST Cancellation, GST Revocation, GST Registration Restoration Assistance. Our team prepares the documents, files the application and follows up until it is approved.

How does the process work?

It takes five steps: a free consultation, a document checklist for your case, filing by our team, follow-up on any department queries, and delivery of the certificate or number.

What documents do I need?

Each service has its own checklist. Most start with the PAN card of the owner or business, proof of business address, a bank statement or cancelled cheque, the Aadhaar card of the authorised signatory and a passport-size photograph.
