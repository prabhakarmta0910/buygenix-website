How to Export A4 Copy Paper from India: Specifications and Buyers

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 7 min read
Last updated 14 October 2026

A4 copy paper is a steady, repeat-order product. Offices, schools, distributors and government suppliers in the Gulf, South Asia and Africa buy it every month. Competition comes from Indonesia, Thailand and Brazil, so Indian exporters win on consistent quality, private label support and reliable delivery.

Quick answer

To export A4 copy paper from India, get an IEC, GST registration with an LUT, and an RCMC from the relevant export promotion council. A4 sheets fall under HS code 4802 56. Agree the GSM, brightness, whiteness, opacity and packing, approve a sample, load the paper on pallets in dry containers, and quote per box or per tonne on FOB or CIF terms.

Registrations you need

  • IEC, GST and LUT: see the IEC guide and the LUT guide.
  • RCMC: paper products have an export promotion council, and the RCMC is needed for incentives. Our RCMC guide explains which council fits your product.

HS code and specifications

Uncoated writing and printing paper in sheets falls under heading 4802. A4 sheets (210 x 297 mm) weighing 40 to 150 g/m² fall under 4802 56. Confirm the 8-digit code with your customs broker; our HS code guide explains how.

  • GSM: 70, 75 and 80 are the common grades. Many buyers in the Gulf and Africa ask for 70 or 75 GSM to keep the price down, while corporate buyers prefer 80 GSM.
  • Optical properties: brightness (ISO or CIE whiteness), opacity and shade.
  • Performance: smoothness, moisture, curl and jam-free running in copiers and laser printers.
  • Pulp: virgin wood pulp, recycled or a mix. Some buyers ask for FSC or PEFC certified paper.

Packing and private label

A4 paper is packed 500 sheets to a ream, usually 5 reams to a box, and boxes are stacked on pallets. Ream wrappers need to be moisture resistant. Many importers sell under their own brand, so agree the ream wrapper and box artwork, barcodes and pallet labels before production. Keep a signed copy of the approved artwork.

Shipping and documents

Paper is heavy, so a 20 ft container usually reaches its weight limit before it fills its volume. Plan the load with your forwarder and the shipping line's weight limits, and use pallets and dunnage so the boxes do not move. Our container size guide covers the limits. You will need a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin; see the documents checklist. Gulf buyers often ask for a certificate of origin so they can claim preferential duty under CEPA; see our India-UAE CEPA guide.

Pricing and payment

Quote per box or per metric tonne, FOB or CIF, and state the GSM, brightness and brand. Pulp prices move, so keep the validity short. Use the free export price calculator to check the figures. For new buyers, take an advance or a letter of credit.

Where to find A4 paper buyers

The UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Kenya and other African markets buy Indian A4 paper, as do distributors in the UK and the USA. On BuyGenix you can see live A4 copy paper requirements and country pages such as A4 paper buyers in Saudi Arabia, Sri Lanka and the UK.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HS code for A4 copy paper?

A4 uncoated copy paper weighing 40 to 150 g/m² falls under HS code 4802 56.

Which GSM of A4 paper sells most in export?

70 and 75 GSM sell well in price-sensitive markets such as the Gulf and Africa, while 80 GSM is preferred by corporate buyers.

How is A4 paper packed for export?

Usually 500 sheets per ream, 5 reams per box, with boxes stacked on pallets and loaded in dry containers.

Can I export A4 paper under the buyer's brand?

Yes. Private label is common. Agree and sign off the ream wrapper and box artwork before production.
