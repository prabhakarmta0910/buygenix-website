What we handle in ICEGATE & Customs

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

ICEGATE Registration

ICEGATE registration is required before you can file shipping bills and duty-related transactions through India's customs portal — a prerequisite for any exporter's first shipment.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your ICEGATE registration so your customs filings are ready to go from your very first shipment.

ICEGATE registration guide

ICEGATE DSC Registration

Linking your Digital Signature Certificate to ICEGATE is required for authenticated customs filings, and a mismatch here is a common technical blocker.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your ICEGATE DSC registration correctly so your filings aren't rejected on technical grounds.

Digital Signature Certificate guide

ICEGATE Profile Update

Keeping your ICEGATE profile details current avoids mismatches that can delay shipment processing.

BuyGenix Solutions updates your ICEGATE profile whenever your business details change.

Port Registration

Registering your business at a specific customs port is required before you can file shipments from that location — a step many first-time exporters overlook until their shipment is ready.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your port registration well ahead of your planned shipment date.

Multiple Port Registration

Exporters shipping from more than one location need separate port registrations for each — a requirement that catches out businesses expanding to a new port or airport.

BuyGenix Solutions registers your business across every port you plan to ship from, in advance of when you'll need it.

AD Code Registration

AD Code registration for exporters is mandatory before filing any shipping bill at a given port, and it must be repeated separately at every new port you use — a detail that trips up many exporters.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your AD Code registration at every port relevant to your shipping plans.

AD code registration guide

AD Code Amendment

Amending your AD Code is necessary after a change in your export banking arrangement, and an outdated AD Code can hold up shipping bill filing.

BuyGenix Solutions updates your AD Code promptly whenever your banking details change.

Bank Account Registration

Registering your export bank account details on ICEGATE supports accurate duty and refund processing tied to your shipments.

BuyGenix Solutions ensures your bank account details are correctly registered on ICEGATE.

Shipping Bill Documentation Assistance

Complete, accurate supporting documents behind your shipping bill are what keep customs clearance moving without a query or hold.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares the full document set your shipping bill needs to clear customs smoothly.

Shipping Bill Data Preparation

Correctly formatted shipment data is essential for shipping bill filing, since even small data errors can trigger a customs query.

BuyGenix Solutions compiles and formats your shipment data accurately before filing.

Shipping Bill Amendment Assistance

Filing a correction to a shipping bill after an error is identified needs to be handled promptly to avoid delaying your shipment further.

BuyGenix Solutions files shipping bill amendments quickly to minimise disruption to your export schedule.

Shipping Bill Tracking

Tracking your shipping bill status through customs clearance gives you visibility into exactly where a shipment stands and whether action is needed.

BuyGenix Solutions monitors your shipping bill status and flags any action needed on your end.

Let Export Order Documentation

The Let Export Order is the customs clearance confirming your goods can be shipped, and its documentation needs to be complete and consistent with your other filings.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your Let Export Order documentation to keep your clearance process moving smoothly.

Bill of Export Assistance

A Bill of Export is required for shipments to SEZs or under specific export schemes, and it has different documentation requirements from a standard shipping bill.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your Bill of Export correctly for SEZ or scheme-linked shipments.

Customs Query Reply Assistance

A well-drafted, prompt reply to a customs query can be the difference between quick clearance and a prolonged shipment hold.

BuyGenix Solutions drafts clear, complete replies to customs queries to keep your shipment moving.

Customs Documentation Review

A pre-submission review of your export documentation catches errors before customs does, avoiding queries and hold-ups altogether.

BuyGenix Solutions reviews your complete export documentation set before it reaches customs.

Export General Manifest (EGM) Follow-up

Following up on EGM filing by your carrier matters because your export isn't considered complete for refund purposes until the EGM is properly filed.

BuyGenix Solutions follows up on EGM filing so your refund claims aren't held up by a carrier delay.

Export Documentation Checklist

A product-specific export documentation checklist ensures nothing is missed before your shipment date, rather than discovering a gap at the port.

BuyGenix Solutions provides a complete, product-specific documentation checklist tailored to your exact shipment.

CHA/Customs Documentation Coordination

Coordinating between you and your Customs House Agent keeps your shipment documentation consistent, since gaps between exporter and CHA paperwork are a common cause of delay.

BuyGenix Solutions coordinates directly with your CHA to keep your documentation aligned and consistent.

Export Shipment File Preparation

A complete, organised shipment file — invoice, packing list, shipping bill, and certificates together — makes customs clearance, bank realisation, and any future audit far smoother.

BuyGenix Solutions compiles your complete export shipment file so everything you need is organised in one place.
