📋 ICEGATE & Customs

ICEGATE & Customs

Getting a shipment through customs cleanly depends on a chain of registrations and documents working together — ICEGATE access, AD Code, and accurate shipping bills among them. Here's what our 20 ICEGATE and customs services cover.

← All Registration Categories
20 Services in This Category

ICEGATE Registration

Registers your business on ICEGATE, the customs portal used for filing shipping bills and duty-related transactions.

ICEGATE DSC Registration

Links your Digital Signature Certificate to ICEGATE for authenticated customs filings.

ICEGATE Profile Update

Updates your business details on your existing ICEGATE profile.

Port Registration

Registers your business at a specific customs port so you can file shipments from there.

Multiple Port Registration

Registers your business across several ports for exporters who ship from more than one location.

AD Code Registration

Registers your bank's Authorised Dealer Code at a port, mandatory before filing any shipping bill there.

AD Code Amendment

Updates AD Code details, such as after a change in your export banking arrangement.

Bank Account Registration

Registers your export bank account details on ICEGATE for duty and refund processing.

Shipping Bill Documentation Assistance

Prepares the supporting documents needed to file an accurate, complete shipping bill.

Shipping Bill Data Preparation

Compiles and formats shipment data correctly for shipping bill filing.

Shipping Bill Amendment Assistance

Files corrections to a shipping bill after an error is identified post-filing.

Shipping Bill Tracking

Tracks the status of your filed shipping bill through customs clearance.

Let Export Order Documentation

Prepares documentation around the Let Export Order, the customs clearance confirming your goods can be shipped.

Bill of Export Assistance

Prepares the Bill of Export required for shipments to SEZs or under specific export schemes.

Customs Query Reply Assistance

Drafts responses to customs department queries raised during shipment clearance.

Customs Documentation Review

Reviews your export documentation set for accuracy before it's submitted to customs.

Export General Manifest (EGM) Follow-up

Follows up on EGM filing by the carrier, needed for your export to be considered complete for refund purposes.

Export Documentation Checklist

Provides a complete, product-specific checklist of documents needed for your shipment.

CHA/Customs Documentation Coordination

Coordinates between you and your Customs House Agent to keep shipment documentation consistent and complete.

Export Shipment File Preparation

Compiles the complete shipment file — invoice, packing list, shipping bill, and certificates — in one organised set.
