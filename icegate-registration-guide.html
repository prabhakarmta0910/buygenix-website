ICEGATE Registration for Exporters: Complete Guide

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read
Last updated 6 October 2026
Quick answer

ICEGATE (Indian Customs Electronic Gateway) is the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs portal for electronic customs filing. Exporters use it to file or track shipping bills, check the status of IGST refunds and duty drawback, register bank accounts for refunds and pay customs duty online. Registration is free and needs your IEC, PAN, contact details and a digital signature for some services.

What ICEGATE is used for

  • Filing and tracking shipping bills and bills of entry, directly or through a customs broker
  • Checking IGST refund status on exports and resolving errors
  • Tracking duty drawback and RoDTEP claims
  • Registering and updating the bank account for customs refunds
  • Paying customs duty online and downloading e-documents

Who should register

Every IEC holder who exports or imports regularly should register, even if a customs broker files the shipping bills. It is the easiest way to see the status of your own refunds and shipments.

Documents and details needed

  • IEC and PAN of the business
  • Email address and mobile number of the authorised person
  • Digital signature certificate (DSC) for services that need signing
  • Bank details and AD code, for refund-related services

How to register

  1. Open the ICEGATE portalChoose new registration and select the user type, for example IEC holder.
  2. Enter IEC and contact detailsVerify your email and mobile number with the OTPs sent.
  3. Add the DSC if neededLink a digital signature for services that require it.
  4. Activate your accountOnce verified, log in to access e-filing, tracking and payments.

Frequently asked questions

Is ICEGATE registration mandatory for exporters?

Not always, because a customs broker can file on your behalf, but it is strongly recommended so you can track refunds and shipments yourself.

Is there a fee for ICEGATE registration?

No. Registration on ICEGATE is free.

Can I check IGST refund status on ICEGATE?

Yes. Exporters can see IGST refund status and errors against their shipping bills.

Do I need a digital signature for ICEGATE?

Some services require a DSC. Basic registration and tracking can be done with OTP verification.
