Importing from India to Netherlands: Trade Compliance Guide for Netherlands Buyers

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read

If your business in Netherlands is exploring India as a sourcing destination, the products are usually the easy part — it's the customs, documentation, and compliance side that trips up first-time importers. Here's what Netherlands buyers actually need to know before placing that first order.

Why Netherlands Buyers Are Looking to India

The Netherlands' Port of Rotterdam is Europe's largest port and a major re-export and distribution hub, making Dutch importers a gateway for Indian goods reaching the wider European market. For Netherlands businesses looking to diversify suppliers, reduce costs, or access categories India specialises in, working directly with Indian exporters — rather than through multiple layers of trading intermediaries — is increasingly the more competitive option.

Customs & Duty Considerations

Imports from India into Netherlands are cleared through Dutch Customs (Douane), operating under the EU Customs Union. as an EU member state, the Netherlands will benefit from the EU-India Free Trade Agreement once it's ratified and implemented, following the conclusion of negotiations in January 2026 Before finalising pricing with an Indian supplier, confirm the exact duty treatment for your product's specific HS code — assuming a general rate based on the product category alone is one of the most common, and costly, mistakes new importers make.

Documents You Should Expect From a Compliant Indian Exporter

A genuinely compliant Indian exporter will readily provide a valid IEC (Import Export Code), GST registration details, a commercial invoice matching your purchase order exactly, a packing list, and a Certificate of Origin where relevant to your trade terms. If a supplier is reluctant to share these, or the documents don't match what they've verbally quoted, treat that as a warning sign rather than a minor inconsistency.

Common Product Categories Netherlands Imports from India

Netherlands businesses most commonly import textiles, agricultural products, chemicals, machinery, and processed foods from India, though sourcing patterns continue to diversify as Indian manufacturing capacity expands across engineering, electronics, and specialty chemicals as well.
