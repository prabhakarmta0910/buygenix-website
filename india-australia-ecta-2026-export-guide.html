India-Australia ECTA: Export Opportunities and Compliance Guide for 2026

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read

The India-Australia Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA) has quietly become one of the strongest-performing trade deals in India's portfolio, with exports under the agreement reaching $7.28 billion in FY 2026 — a sharp jump from a market where Certificates of Origin barely existed before the deal.

How Much ECTA Has Grown Trade

Since ECTA became operational in December 2022, over 2.73 lakh Certificates of Origin have been issued — compared to just 1,482 in the year before the agreement came into force. The number of tariff lines Indian exporters actually ship to Australia has grown from 5,396 to 5,668, showing genuine diversification beyond a handful of dominant categories.

Why Australia Is Worth a Closer Look

Australia offers Indian exporters a high-value, English-speaking market with strong existing demand for Indian textiles, pharmaceuticals, engineering goods, and gems and jewellery. ECTA's tariff elimination on a large share of Indian goods has made Indian suppliers meaningfully more price-competitive against exporters from countries without a comparable agreement with Australia.

Claiming Preferential Treatment Under ECTA

To actually benefit from ECTA's tariff concessions, exporters need a valid Certificate of Origin confirming their goods meet the agreement's Rules of Origin — this isn't automatic, and shipping without one means paying Australia's standard MFN tariff rate instead of the preferential rate ECTA actually offers. Getting your product's origin criteria right before you price a deal avoids the awkward position of having already quoted a rate that assumed a duty saving you can't actually claim.

What This Means If You Haven't Exported to Australia Before

  • Confirm your product's specific tariff line and whether it qualifies for ECTA preferential treatment
  • Check the Rules of Origin criteria for your product category before finalising pricing
  • Prepare a Certificate of Origin application through the correct issuing authority in India
  • Factor Australian labelling and import documentation requirements into your export paperwork
