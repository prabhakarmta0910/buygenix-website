India-Oman CEPA 2026: New Free Trade Agreement Guide for Exporters

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 5 min read

India's newest trade agreement is already showing results. The India-Oman Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) came into force on 1 June 2026, and in the very first quarter after implementation, India's exports to Oman grew 26.4% — one of the strongest early responses among India's recent trade deals.

Why This Agreement Matters Right Now

Because CEPA with Oman is so new, most Indian exporters haven't yet built it into their pricing or market strategy — which means there's a genuine first-mover advantage available to businesses that move early. Early-stage trade agreements often see the fastest relative growth as awareness spreads and exporters start actively using the preferential terms, rather than treating Oman as an afterthought within the broader Gulf market.

Oman's Position in the Gulf Trade Landscape

Oman sits alongside the UAE as part of India's growing Gulf trade relationships, and follows the same broader pattern seen with CEPA agreements — tariff reduction on a large share of tariff lines, paired with simplified documentation for exporters who take the time to understand the Rules of Origin requirements. Given Oman's strategic Gulf location and existing logistics infrastructure, it can also serve as a stepping-stone market for Indian exporters looking to build a wider Gulf Cooperation Council presence.

What to Check Before Your First Oman Shipment

  • Confirm whether your specific product's tariff line is covered under the agreement's initial phase
  • Understand the Rules of Origin criteria required to claim preferential duty treatment
  • Prepare a Certificate of Origin through the correct Indian issuing authority
  • Check Oman-specific import documentation and labelling requirements, since these can differ from other Gulf markets even where tariff treatment is similar
