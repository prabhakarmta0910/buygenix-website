International Filings & Registrations

Selling abroad often needs a few registrations in the buyer's country: an FDA registration for food going to the USA, an EORI number for UK or EU customs, a D-U-N-S number buyers can check, or a company and tax ID overseas. None of these need a CA, CS or lawyer. Here is what our 15 international services cover and how BuyGenix Solutions handles each one.

What we handle in International Filings & Registrations

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

US FDA Food Facility Registration

Any facility that manufactures, processes, packs or holds food for consumption in the USA must register with the US Food and Drug Administration before shipping. Registration is free, it must be renewed every two years between October and December of even-numbered years, and a foreign facility has to name a US Agent.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares and files your FDA registration and renewal and helps you appoint a US Agent. We do not act as the US Agent ourselves.

US FDA Prior Notice Filing

Every food shipment to the USA needs a Prior Notice submitted to FDA before it arrives, within time limits set for each mode of transport. The confirmation number has to travel with the shipment, and a missing or late notice can get the cargo held at the port.

BuyGenix Solutions files Prior Notice for each shipment and shares the confirmation number with you and your freight forwarder.

D-U-N-S Number

A D-U-N-S number is a free nine-digit business ID issued by Dun & Bradstreet. Many overseas buyers, marketplaces and portals ask for it to check a supplier, and FDA uses it as the facility identifier for food facility registration.

BuyGenix Solutions applies for your D-U-N-S number and makes sure your company details match your other records.

US LLC Formation

A US Limited Liability Company lets an Indian exporter invoice, hold a US bank account and sell on US marketplaces as a local business. Non-residents can form an LLC with a US state such as Wyoming or Delaware through a registered agent in that state.

BuyGenix Solutions forms your LLC, arranges the registered agent and prepares the formation documents.

US LLC registration guide

US EIN (Employer Identification Number)

An EIN is the US federal tax ID for your LLC, needed for a US bank account, payment platforms and tax filings. Owners without a US Social Security Number apply to the IRS with Form SS-4.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares and submits your Form SS-4 and follows up with the IRS until the EIN is issued.

UK Company Formation

A UK private limited company is registered with Companies House online, and non-UK residents can be directors and shareholders. You need a UK registered office address, which is often provided by a formation agent.

BuyGenix Solutions registers your UK company and arranges the registered office address.

UK EORI Number

An EORI number is needed to move goods into or out of Great Britain. If you ship on terms where you act as the importer, such as DDP, your business needs its own UK EORI number.

BuyGenix Solutions checks whether you need a UK EORI number and applies for it.

EU EORI Number

For EU customs, a business outside the EU gets its EORI number from the customs authority of the EU country where it first lodges a customs declaration. You need one when you act as the importer of record in the EU.

BuyGenix Solutions identifies the right EU country for your EORI application and files it.

REX Registration

The Registered Exporter (REX) system lets exporters in GSP beneficiary countries, including India, issue their own statement on origin so buyers in the EU, Norway, Switzerland and Turkey can claim lower duty on products that still qualify. Indian exporters register through the DGFT portal.

BuyGenix Solutions checks whether your products qualify and completes your REX registration.

GS1 Barcode Registration

International retailers and marketplaces ask for GS1 barcodes (GTINs) on every product. GS1 India issues your company prefix and barcodes, and it charges its own membership fee.

BuyGenix Solutions completes your GS1 application and helps you assign barcodes to each product and pack size.

Halal Certification Application

Food, cosmetics and some other products sold to buyers in the Gulf and South-East Asia often need halal certification from a body the importing country accepts. The certification body audits your process before issuing the certificate.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you choose a certification body your buyer's country accepts and prepares the application and documents for the audit.

Amazon Global Selling Account Setup

Amazon Global Selling lets Indian businesses sell on Amazon marketplaces such as the USA, UK and UAE. Each marketplace needs a verified seller account with business, bank and tax details.

BuyGenix Solutions sets up your seller account and prepares the documents Amazon asks for during verification.

International Payment Account Setup

Business accounts with platforms such as PayPal, Payoneer and Wise let overseas buyers pay you in their own currency. Each platform checks your business documents before approving the account.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you open and verify the accounts that suit your buyers and keeps the documents consistent with your bank records.

ECGC Export Credit Insurance Application

ECGC (Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India) policies protect you if an overseas buyer does not pay because of default, insolvency or problems in the buyer's country. You can also apply for a credit limit on a specific buyer before you ship.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you choose the right ECGC policy and prepares the proposal and buyer credit limit applications.

CE Marking Documentation

Many products sold in the EU need a CE mark. For most of them the manufacturer issues an EU Declaration of Conformity and keeps a technical file, while some product types also need testing or approval by a notified body.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your Declaration of Conformity and technical file. Any required testing is done by an accredited lab or notified body.
