What we handle in Legal Metrology & Labelling

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

Legal Metrology Registration Assistance

Legal Metrology registration governs weights, measures, and packaged goods compliance, and it's a requirement many businesses discover only after a shipment is queried.

BuyGenix Solutions registers your business under Legal Metrology rules correctly from the outset.

LMPC Registration Assistance

The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules govern how packaged products must be labelled and declared, and registration here is required for most packaged exports.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your LMPC registration and ongoing packaged-commodity compliance.

Importer/Manufacturer Declaration Assistance

The mandatory declaration required from importers and manufacturers under packaging rules needs to be accurate and consistent across your product range.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your importer/manufacturer declaration to meet Legal Metrology requirements.

Packaged Commodity Compliance

Reviewing your packaged product against Legal Metrology labelling and declaration requirements before it ships avoids a costly relabelling exercise later.

BuyGenix Solutions reviews your packaged product for full Legal Metrology compliance before export.

Product Label Review

A pre-shipment review of your product label against applicable regulatory requirements catches issues before they become a customs or retailer rejection.

BuyGenix Solutions reviews your product label thoroughly before it goes into production or ships.

MRP/Net Quantity Label Review

Checking that MRP and net quantity declarations meet legal requirements protects you from compliance flags that are entirely avoidable with a pre-shipment check.

BuyGenix Solutions verifies your MRP and net quantity labelling meets all applicable requirements.

Country of Origin Label Review

Country-of-origin labelling rules differ by destination market, and getting this wrong is a surprisingly common reason for shipments being held at customs.

BuyGenix Solutions confirms your country-of-origin labelling meets your destination market's specific rules.

Export Packaging Label Review

Reviewing export packaging labels for compliance with both Indian and destination-market rules covers a gap that purely domestic labelling reviews miss.

BuyGenix Solutions reviews your export packaging against both Indian and destination-country requirements.

Barcode/Label Documentation Assistance

Barcode and label documentation is required for retail-ready export shipments, particularly when selling into organised retail chains abroad.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares the barcode and label documentation your retail-ready shipment needs.

Product Compliance Checklist

A consolidated checklist covering labelling, packaging, and declarations gives you one clear reference instead of checking multiple regulations separately.

BuyGenix Solutions provides a consolidated compliance checklist covering every labelling and packaging requirement for your product.

Which Legal Metrology & Labelling services does BuyGenix handle?

How does the process work?

It takes five steps: a free consultation, a document checklist for your case, filing by our team, follow-up on any department queries, and delivery of the certificate or number.

What documents do I need?

Each service has its own checklist. Most start with the PAN card of the owner or business, proof of business address, a bank statement or cancelled cheque, the Aadhaar card of the authorised signatory and a passport-size photograph. Companies and firms also need an authorisation letter and a Digital Signature Certificate. We share the exact list once we know your business.
