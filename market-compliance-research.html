Market & Compliance Research

Before you ship to a new market, you need to know what that market actually requires — classification, labelling, testing, and non-tariff barriers included. Here's what our 13 market and compliance research services cover, and how BuyGenix Solutions helps you enter new markets with confidence.

← All Registration Categories
13 Services in This Category

HS Code Research

Correct HS code research determines your product's tariff treatment, export policy, and scheme eligibility — misclassification is one of the most expensive mistakes an exporter can make.

BuyGenix Solutions researches and confirms the correct HS code for your product before you commit to pricing or shipping.

Product Classification

Accurate product classification against tariff and regulatory schedules underpins nearly every other export decision you'll make.

BuyGenix Solutions classifies your product correctly against the relevant tariff and regulatory schedules.

Country Import Requirement Research

Understanding exactly what your buyer's country requires to import your product avoids shipments getting stuck at the destination port.

BuyGenix Solutions researches your buyer's country-specific import requirements before you finalise a shipment.

Destination Country Compliance Checklist

A compliance checklist tailored to your shipment's destination country turns a vague sense of what might be needed into a concrete action list.

BuyGenix Solutions builds a destination-specific compliance checklist for every new market you enter.

Export Prohibited/Restricted Product Check

Checking whether your product faces export restrictions or prohibitions under current policy protects you from a shipment being blocked outright.

BuyGenix Solutions checks your product against current export restrictions before you commit to a sale.

Importer Requirement Checklist

Compiling what your buyer needs on their end to import your product smoothly helps you proactively support the transaction rather than reacting to problems.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you understand and support your buyer's import-side requirements.

Product Labelling Requirement Research

Labelling rules vary significantly by destination market, and getting them wrong is a common reason shipments are rejected or relabelled at the port.

BuyGenix Solutions researches the exact labelling requirements your product must meet in its destination market.

Packaging Compliance Research

Packaging standards required for your product in the destination market often go beyond what's needed for domestic sale.

BuyGenix Solutions researches destination-specific packaging compliance requirements for your product.

Certificate Requirement Research

Identifying which certificates your product needs for a specific destination market avoids the scramble to obtain one after a shipment is already booked.

BuyGenix Solutions identifies every certificate your product needs well ahead of your shipment date.

Testing Requirement Research

Identifying which lab tests your product must pass for a specific destination market lets you plan testing timelines into your export schedule.

BuyGenix Solutions researches the specific testing requirements your product needs to clear its destination market.

Non-Tariff Barrier Research

Non-tariff barriers — standards, quotas, or restrictions — can affect a shipment just as much as a tariff, and they're often less visible until you research a specific market.

BuyGenix Solutions researches non-tariff barriers that could affect your shipment to a specific market.

Market Entry Compliance Report

A compliance report covering everything needed before entering a new export market consolidates research that would otherwise be scattered across many sources.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares a complete market entry compliance report before you commit to a new market.

Country-wise Export Documentation Report

A documentation report tailored to a specific country's import requirements gives you a single reference point instead of piecing together rules from multiple sources.

BuyGenix Solutions prepares a country-specific documentation report for each new export destination you target.
