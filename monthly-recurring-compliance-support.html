Monthly & Recurring Compliance: common questions

Which Monthly & Recurring Compliance services does BuyGenix handle?

We handle 15 services in this category, including Monthly GST Filing, Quarterly GST Filing, Monthly Export Documentation Support, Monthly Compliance Monitoring, RCMC Expiry Monitoring. Our team prepares the documents, files the application and follows up until it is approved.

How does the process work?

It takes five steps: a free consultation, a document checklist for your case, filing by our team, follow-up on any department queries, and delivery of the certificate or number.

What documents do I need?

Each service has its own checklist. Most start with the PAN card of the owner or business, proof of business address, a bank statement or cancelled cheque, the Aadhaar card of the authorised signatory and a passport-size photograph. Companies and firms also need an authorisation letter and a Digital Signature Certificate. We share the exact list once we know your business.

Are these services included in a BuyGenix membership?

Membership plans include selected registrations: IEC and MSME in every plan and GST from the Growth plan. The Enterprise plan also includes 2 months of GST return filing. For anything outside your plan, our team tells you the cost before starting.
