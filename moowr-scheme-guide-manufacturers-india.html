MOOWR Scheme Explained: Duty-Free Manufacturing in a Bonded Warehouse

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 7 min read
Last updated 2 October 2026

If your business imports raw materials or capital goods for manufacturing, paying customs duty upfront can tie up significant working capital before you've even sold a single unit. The MOOWR scheme (Manufacture and Other Operations in Warehouse Regulations) was designed to solve exactly this problem — and it's one of the most underused schemes among Indian manufacturers today.

Quick answer

MOOWR (Manufacture and Other Operations in Warehouse Regulations, 2019) lets a manufacturer import raw materials and capital goods into a customs-bonded unit without paying import duty upfront. Duty is paid only when finished goods are cleared into the Indian market, and no duty is paid on inputs used in goods that are exported. There is no export obligation, no minimum investment, and the licence does not need periodic renewal.

What is the MOOWR Scheme

MOOWR, notified in 2019 under Section 58 and Section 65 of the Customs Act, 1962, allows businesses to import raw materials and capital goods into a customs-bonded warehouse without paying import duty upfront. Manufacturing, assembly, processing, or packaging can then take place inside that same bonded premises, and duty becomes payable only when the finished goods are cleared into the domestic market — not when goods are exported.

Key Benefits of the MOOWR Scheme for Manufacturers

  • 100% duty deferment on imported raw materials and capital goods at the time of import
  • No customs duty at all on inputs used in goods that are ultimately exported
  • No export obligation — unlike EOU or Advance Authorisation, you can sell into the domestic market too
  • No investment threshold to set up a MOOWR unit
  • Perpetual licence validity — no periodic renewal required
  • No upper time limit on how long imported goods can be stored in the bonded warehouse

Who is Eligible to Apply for a MOOWR Licence

Any business engaged in manufacturing or other approved operations can apply, provided it has suitable premises that can be notified as a private bonded warehouse, maintains proper inventory and accounting records, and is able to comply with ongoing Customs supervision and reporting requirements.

Documents Required and the MOOWR Application Process

The application is filed with the jurisdictional Customs Commissioner and broadly covers three parts: business constitution and registered office details, details of the proposed bonded facility (including whether it's an existing warehouse or Authorized Economic Operator), and applicant undertakings. Once documents are verified and the premises inspected, the private bonded warehouse licence combined with manufacturing permission is issued — a single application covers both approvals.

MOOWR vs EOU vs SEZ — How Do They Compare

Unlike an EOU (Export Oriented Unit) or operating inside an SEZ (Special Economic Zone), MOOWR carries no export obligation — you're free to sell your finished goods domestically as well as internationally. MOOWR units also don't need to be located inside any designated economic zone, and setup has no minimum investment threshold, making it considerably more flexible for manufacturers who want duty deferment benefits without committing to export-only operations.

For import-heavy manufacturers, MOOWR can meaningfully improve cash flow and reduce the cost of setting up production in India — but getting the licence application and ongoing compliance right matters, since Customs supervision and reporting requirements continue for as long as the unit operates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOOWR scheme?

MOOWR is a customs scheme that lets businesses import raw materials and capital goods into a private bonded warehouse without paying duty upfront, manufacture or process them there, and pay duty only when finished goods are cleared for sale in India.

Is there an export obligation under MOOWR?

No. MOOWR has no export obligation. You can sell finished goods in India (paying duty on the imported inputs at clearance) or export them (with no duty on those inputs).

Who can apply for a MOOWR licence?

Any business engaged in manufacturing or other approved operations that has premises suitable to be notified as a private bonded warehouse, keeps proper inventory and accounting records, and can comply with customs supervision.

How do I apply for MOOWR?

Apply to the jurisdictional Customs Commissioner with business, premises and undertaking details. After document checks and a premises inspection, a single licence covers both the private bonded warehouse and the manufacturing permission.

How is MOOWR different from EOU and SEZ?

MOOWR has no export obligation, no minimum investment and no requirement to be inside a designated zone, so it is more flexible for manufacturers who sell both in India and abroad.
