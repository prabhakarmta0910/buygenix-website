What we handle in RCMC & Export Promotion Councils

Each service below is handled end to end by our team. Pick what you need, or ask us to check which ones apply to your business.

APEDA RCMC

An APEDA RCMC is mandatory for exporting agricultural and processed food products from India, and it's one of the most frequently required registrations for agri exporters.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your APEDA RCMC registration end-to-end, including the correct product category selection.

RCMC registration guide

APEDA RCMC Amendment

Amending your APEDA RCMC — for product categories or business information — keeps your registration aligned with your actual export activity.

BuyGenix Solutions files your APEDA RCMC amendment accurately and promptly.

APEDA RCMC Renewal/Related Assistance

Your APEDA RCMC needs periodic renewal to stay valid, and a lapsed RCMC can block your eligibility for export incentives tied to it.

BuyGenix Solutions tracks your APEDA RCMC renewal deadline so your scheme eligibility is never interrupted.

FIEO RCMC

FIEO RCMC is the general-purpose export council registration for products without a dedicated commodity board, making it relevant to a wide range of exporters.

BuyGenix Solutions registers your business with FIEO when your product doesn't fall under a specific commodity board.

FIEO RCMC guide

Spices Board Registration

Spices Board registration is mandatory for anyone exporting spices from India, and it's typically one of the first steps for a new spice export business.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your Spices Board registration and the documentation it requires.

Spices Board registration guide

Tea Board Registration

Tea Board registration is required for businesses engaged in tea export and trade, tying directly into scheme eligibility for this product category.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your Tea Board registration process from application to certificate.

Tea Board registration guide

Coffee Board Registration

Coffee Board registration is required for coffee exporters and traders, and it connects to several coffee-specific export benefits.

BuyGenix Solutions handles your Coffee Board registration and keeps it aligned with your export activity.

MPEDA Registration

MPEDA registration is mandatory for exporting marine and seafood products, and it comes with specific traceability and quality requirements.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your MPEDA registration and helps you understand its ongoing compliance requirements.

MPEDA registration guide

BuyGenix Solutions registers your engineering export business with EEPC India correctly the first time.

BuyGenix Solutions updates your APEDA registration whenever your product range expands.

RCMC Product Addition

Updating your RCMC to include additional product categories keeps your council registration matched to your actual export activity.

BuyGenix Solutions files your RCMC product addition so your registration stays current with your business.

RCMC Modification

General RCMC modifications — address, ownership, or other changes — need to be filed with your registered council to stay valid.

BuyGenix Solutions handles RCMC modifications with whichever council your business is registered under.

RCMC Issuance Assistance

Managing the RCMC application from start to finish with the correct export promotion council avoids delays from choosing the wrong body.

BuyGenix Solutions manages your complete RCMC application and issuance process, council selection included.

Export Promotion Council Selection

Choosing the correct Export Promotion Council for your specific product is essential — an RCMC from the wrong body may not be accepted for the scheme benefits you're trying to claim.

BuyGenix Solutions helps you identify exactly which council your product category should register under.

RCMC Documentation Preparation

BuyGenix Solutions prepares your complete RCMC documentation so your application isn't delayed by missing paperwork.

Which RCMC & Export Promotion Councils services does BuyGenix handle?

How does the process work?

It takes five steps: a free consultation, a document checklist for your case, filing by our team, follow-up on any department queries, and delivery of the certificate or number.

What documents do I need?

Each service has its own checklist. Most start with the PAN card of the owner or business, proof of business address, a bank statement or cancelled cheque, the Aadhaar card of the authorised signatory and a passport-size photograph. Companies and firms also need an authorisation letter and a Digital Signature Certificate. We share the exact list once we know your business.
