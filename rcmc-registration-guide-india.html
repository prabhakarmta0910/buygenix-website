RCMC Registration in India: Complete Guide for Exporters

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read

If you plan to claim export incentives, benefit from government export promotion schemes, or simply want credibility in front of international buyers, you'll eventually run into one requirement: the RCMC (Registration-Cum-Membership Certificate). Here's what it is and how to get one.

What is RCMC and Why Exporters Need It

An RCMC certifies that your business is a registered member of the relevant Export Promotion Council (EPC) or Commodity Board for your product category. Many DGFT benefits, duty concessions, and export incentive schemes require a valid RCMC as a prerequisite — without it, you may be excluded from schemes your business would otherwise qualify for.

How to Choose the Right Export Promotion Council for Your Product

India has dozens of product-specific Export Promotion Councils and Boards — for example, APEDA for agricultural and processed food products, Spices Board for spices, Tea Board and Coffee Board for their respective commodities, MPEDA for marine products, and EEPC India for engineering goods. If your product doesn't fall under any commodity-specific board, FIEO (Federation of Indian Export Organisations) acts as the council of last resort for general exporters. Choosing the correct council matters — an RCMC from the wrong body may not be accepted for scheme benefits tied to your actual product category.

Documents Required for RCMC Registration

  • Valid IEC (Import Export Code) certificate
  • PAN card of the business
  • Business registration proof (Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, or GST registration)
  • Bank certificate or cancelled cheque
  • Self-certified declaration of the products to be exported
  • Proof of business address

Step-by-Step RCMC Application Process

  • Step 1: Identify the correct Export Promotion Council or Commodity Board for your product category.
  • Step 2: Apply online through the respective council's portal (or the common DGFT RCMC portal for FIEO and several other bodies) with your IEC and business documents.
  • Step 3: Pay the applicable membership and registration fee, which varies by council and business turnover slab.
  • Step 4: Once verified, the council issues the RCMC, typically listing the specific product categories you're registered to export.

RCMC Validity and Renewal

Most RCMCs are valid for five financial years from the year of issue and need to be renewed thereafter. If your product range expands into a new category, you may also need to get that category added to your existing RCMC rather than waiting for renewal.

Holding the correct RCMC keeps your business eligible for the export incentive schemes and council-backed trade fair opportunities that are otherwise closed off to unregistered exporters.
