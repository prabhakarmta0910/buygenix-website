Documents

What you'll typically need

Each registration has its own checklist, but most start with the same core documents. For an individual or proprietorship this usually means:

  • PAN card of the owner or business
  • Proof of business address
  • Bank statement or cancelled cheque
  • Aadhaar card of the authorised signatory
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Business registration certificate (if any)
  • Authorisation letter (companies and firms)
  • Digital Signature Certificate (companies)

Board-specific registrations (APEDA, Spices Board, MPEDA, Tea, Coffee and Tobacco Boards) and certificates (COO, Phytosanitary, Fumigation) need a few extra product-specific documents. We share the exact list once we know your product.
