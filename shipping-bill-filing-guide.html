Shipping Bill Filing for Exporters: Complete Guide

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read
Last updated 6 October 2026
Quick answer

The shipping bill is the main customs declaration for exporting goods from India. It is filed electronically on the customs system through ICEGATE by the exporter or a customs broker, with details of the exporter, buyer, goods, HS codes, value and the export benefits claimed. After assessment and any examination, customs grants the Let Export Order (LEO), and the shipping bill also acts as the claim for IGST refund and drawback.

ICEGATEWhere shipping bills are filed
LEOLet Export Order from customs
EGMFiled by the carrier after sailing
IGST refundClaimed through the shipping bill

Types of shipping bill

TypeUsed for
Free shipping billGoods with no export duty or benefits claimed
Dutiable shipping billGoods that attract export duty
Drawback shipping billExports claiming duty drawback
Ex-bond shipping billGoods exported from a bonded warehouse

Details needed to file

  • IEC, GSTIN and AD code of the exporter's bank
  • Invoice number, value, currency and Incoterms
  • HS code and description of each item
  • Buyer name and country, ports and container details
  • Declarations for drawback, RoDTEP, and IGST paid or LUT

From filing to export

  1. File the shipping billOn ICEGATE, directly or through a customs broker.
  2. AssessmentCustoms checks the declaration under the risk management system.
  3. Examination if selectedGoods may be examined at the port or container freight station.
  4. Let Export OrderCustoms grants LEO and the goods are loaded.
  5. EGM and refundsAfter the carrier files the export general manifest, IGST refund and drawback are processed.

Common errors that hold up refunds

  • Invoice values in the shipping bill not matching GSTR-1
  • Wrong GSTIN or IEC details
  • Bank account or AD code not registered with customs
  • Missing or wrong drawback and RoDTEP declarations

Frequently asked questions

Who files the shipping bill?

The exporter or a licensed customs broker, electronically on ICEGATE.

What is a Let Export Order?

Customs permission to load the goods for export, granted after assessment and any examination.

How does the shipping bill link to IGST refund?

For exports with payment of IGST, the shipping bill is treated as the refund claim once the EGM is filed and GSTR-1 details match.

Can a shipping bill be amended?

Yes, with customs approval, but corrections after LEO are harder, so check details before filing.
