Trademark Registration in India: Complete Guide for Small Businesses & Exporters

BuyGenix Solutions Team · 6 min read

Your brand name and logo are among the most valuable assets your business will ever own — yet many small businesses and exporters put off trademark registration in India until a competitor or copycat forces the issue. Here's what the process actually involves and why doing it early protects your business as it scales into new markets.

Why Trademark Registration Matters for Growing Businesses

A registered trademark gives you exclusive legal rights to use your brand name and logo across India for the goods or services you're registered under, and gives you legal standing to act against anyone using a similar mark. For exporters in particular, a registered trademark also makes it easier to build brand recognition with repeat international buyers and protects your identity as you expand into e-commerce and trade fair marketing.

Documents Required for Trademark Registration in India

  • Applicant's identity and address proof (PAN, Aadhaar, or passport)
  • Business registration proof (Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, or Udyam registration, where applicable)
  • A clear image of the logo or wordmark to be registered
  • Proof of use of the mark, if already in use (invoices, packaging, website screenshots)
  • Power of Attorney (Form TM-48) authorizing your registration agent to file on your behalf

Step-by-Step Trademark Registration Process

  • Step 1 — Trademark Search: Check the existing trademark database to confirm your desired name or logo isn't already registered or too similar to an existing mark.
  • Step 2 — Choose the Right Class: Trademarks in India are registered under specific classes (1 to 45) based on the type of goods or services. Choosing the correct class is critical — an incorrect class can leave your actual product category unprotected.
  • Step 3 — File the Application: Submit Form TM-A along with the required documents to the Trademark Registry, either as an individual/MSME applicant (lower government fee) or as a regular applicant.
  • Step 4 — Examination: The Registrar examines the application and may raise objections, which need to be responded to within the given timeline.
  • Step 5 — Publication: If cleared, the mark is published in the Trademark Journal for public opposition (4 months).
  • Step 6 — Registration: If no opposition is filed, the trademark is registered and the certificate is issued.

How Long Does Trademark Registration Take in India

While the trademark application (TM-A filing) itself is usually done within a few days once documents are ready, full trademark registration typically takes 8–24 months from filing to certificate issuance, depending on objections and oppositions. However, you can start using the ™ symbol as soon as your application is filed, giving you a documented claim from day one.

Trademark Classes Every Exporter Should Know About

Exporters commonly need to register under classes relevant to their product category (e.g., Class 29/30 for food products and spices, Class 25 for textiles and garments, Class 20 for handicrafts and furniture) as well as Class 35 if they also want to protect their business/trading name for marketing and retail services.
